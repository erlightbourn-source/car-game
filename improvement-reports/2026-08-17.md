## Research grounding
WebSearch: "endless runner mobile game near-miss feedback juice game feel 2026
retention design." Concrete, applicable finding from the sweep: 2026-era endless
runner post-run/game-over screens commonly surface **crash cause** alongside the
score recap (peak speed, coins missed, crash cause, sometimes a quick tip) — a
proven clarity/retention pattern, not a generic platitude. Lane Rush's game-over
screen showed score/coins/combo/daily-best but never *why* the run ended, so a
young player crashing on a cone vs. a car got identical feedback either way —
nothing to learn from between runs. Grounded, specific, and directly actionable
against code already in place (obstacle `type` was already tracked per-collision
in `engine.js`, just never surfaced past the collision-resolution branch).
Sources: [Turbogeek — Why Endless Runner Games Are Still Addictive in 2026](https://turbogeek.org/why-endless-runner-games-are-still-addictive-in-2026/), [Northeastern — Impact of Textual Feedback on Player Retention in Endless Runner Mobile Games](https://repository.library.northeastern.edu/files/neu:m0455d01b/fulltext.pdf).

## Shipped (verified) — `f10bcbf`
**Crash-cause line on the game-over screen.** A short, friendly line under the
"Oops, a bump!" headline naming what ended the run:
- 🚧 "A cone got you — steer around them!"
- 🚧 "Blocked by a barrier!"
- 🚗 "Bumped into a car!"

(Potholes are a soft hazard — they jolt + slow but never end a run, so they never
appear here; the line stays hidden for those cases, matching existing behavior.)

**What changed, concretely:**
- `js/engine.js`: `_die()` now takes an optional `cause` and stores it on
  `this.lastCrashType`; the one call site inside collision resolution now passes
  `o.type` (the obstacle that hit the player). Nothing else in the collision/
  difficulty/scoring logic changed.
- `js/main.js`: a small `CRASH_CAUSE_LABELS` copy map + 4 lines in `showOver()`
  to read `engine.lastCrashType` and toggle the new line.
- `index.html` / `style.css`: one new `<p id="over-cause">` element + a matching
  muted-text CSS rule, styled consistent with the existing `.daily-line` /
  `.combo-earned` idiom already on that screen.
- Cache bumped **`?v=37 → 38`** (all 4 touched files load through it).

**Verified by:**

   `GameEngine`, forced a `cone`, `barrier`, and `car` obstacle into direct
